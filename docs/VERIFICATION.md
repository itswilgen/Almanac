## Visual inspection

Landing, populated/empty journal, calendar, entry dialog, cycle history, and settings were captured and visually inspected near **375px, 768px, and 1440px** (empty-state capture at 375px). Page-width checks found no horizontal page overflow. Dialog screenshots use the viewport rather than full-page capture so their position and backdrop can be assessed accurately. Screenshots: `frontend/test-results/screenshots/` (regenerated by the journey test; ignored by version control). They contain synthetic records only.
